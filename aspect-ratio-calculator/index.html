Free Aspect Ratio Calculator

Fast, free and easy to use. No registration required.

Calculate new dimensions

Enter your original image or video dimensions and a new width to calculate the matching height.

Enter dimensions and select Calculate.

How it works

The calculator multiplies the new width by the original height, then divides by the original width. The result is rounded to the nearest pixel.

Example: 1920 × 1080 resized to a width of 1280 pixels gives a height of 720 pixels.

About ToolNexa

ToolNexa is a growing collection of browser-based utilities for creators. This calculator runs locally in your browser and does not upload files or send your entered dimensions to a server.
