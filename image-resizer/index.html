Resize your photos to the pixel dimensions you need. No upload to a server required.

Resize your image

Choose an image up to 20 MB. Maximum 25 megapixels.

Output format: PNG. Maximum output: 16 megapixels.

How to resize an image

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP file.
  2. Enter the desired width and height. Keep aspect ratio enabled to avoid stretching.
  3. Select Resize & download PNG to save the result.

Does resizing upload my image?

No. This tool uses your browser's image decoding and canvas features. The selected image is not sent to our server by this tool. Large files may require significant device memory. Animated images are not preserved as animations.
